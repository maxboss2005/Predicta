Verse of the Day

"I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."
Philippians 4:13

Today's Meditation

This powerful verse reminds us that our strength doesn't come from ourselves but from Christ. As students facing academic challenges, personal struggles, and the pressures of life, we can find comfort knowing that we're not relying on our own limited abilities. When you feel overwhelmed by exams, relationships, or future uncertainties, remember that Christ provides the strength you need to persevere. Take a moment today to reflect on areas where you need His strength and surrender them in prayer.
