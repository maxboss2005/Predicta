Immediate Help Resources

If you're in crisis or having thoughts of harming yourself:

  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 or 1-800-273-8255
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • Campus Counseling Center: Contact your university's mental health services
  • Emergency Services: Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room

You are not alone. Help is available, and recovery is possible.
