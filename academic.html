Study Tips & Strategies

Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Repeat 4 times, then take a longer break.

Active Recall

Test yourself frequently instead of just re-reading notes. This strengthens memory.

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals to optimize long-term retention.

Teach What You Learn
