Interview Preparation

Practice common interview questions for your field.

1. Tell me about yourself.

Structure your answer: Present → Past → Future. Start with your current role/studies, briefly mention relevant past experience, and connect to your future goals with the company.

Example: "I'm currently a Computer Science student at XYZ University, where I've developed strong programming skills through projects like [mention specific project]. Previously, I interned at ABC Corp where I gained experience in [relevant skill]. I'm excited about this opportunity because..."

2. Why do you want to work here?

Show you've researched the company. Mention specific things about their mission, products, culture, or recent achievements that resonate with you.

Tip: Connect your skills and values to what the company does. "I admire your company's commitment to [specific value], and my experience in [your skill] aligns well with your work on [specific project or product]."

3. What are your strengths and weaknesses?

Strengths: Pick 2-3 relevant to the job. Provide specific examples. "I'm highly organized, which helped me manage multiple projects while maintaining a 3.8 GPA."

Weaknesses: Choose a real but minor weakness. Show self-awareness and mention steps you're taking to improve. "I used to struggle with public speaking, so I joined Toastmasters and now regularly present in class."

Job Search Tools

Resources and strategies for finding opportunities.

Job Search Tips

  • Use specific keywords in your search (e.g., "entry-level," "internship," "remote")
  • Set up job alerts on LinkedIn, Indeed, and company career pages
  • Network with alumni from your university
  • Customize your resume for each application
  • Follow up 1-2 weeks after applying
  • Use Boolean search operators (AND, OR, NOT) for better results
